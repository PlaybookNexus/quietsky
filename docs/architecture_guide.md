Why Quiet Sky Exists

Quiet Sky is an AI-assisted astronomical anomaly detection system.

The project began as an investigation into KIC 8462852 (Tabby's Star), but evolved into a generalized framework capable of comparing hundreds of astronomical targets across multiple wavelengths and survey sources.

The central idea is simple:

Most interesting astronomical discoveries begin as anomalies.

Rather than searching for a specific phenomenon, Quiet Sky attempts to identify objects that differ significantly from comparable populations.

Examples:

stars unusual relative to nearby stars
stars unusual relative to exoplanet hosts
stars unusual relative to variable stars
stars unusual relative to control populations

The project intentionally avoids making claims about extraterrestrial intelligence.

Instead, it attempts to answer:

Which objects deserve additional investigation?

Scientific Philosophy

Quiet Sky does not attempt to prove any hypothesis.

Quiet Sky attempts to prioritize investigation.

The system is intentionally built around a simple principle:

Rare objects deserve attention.

The role of the pipeline is not to determine the cause of unusual behavior.

The role of the pipeline is to identify statistically unusual targets and elevate them for human review.

Long-Term Vision

The ultimate goal is not finding extraterrestrial intelligence.

The ultimate goal is creating a repeatable, defensible methodology for identifying astronomical objects that warrant closer scientific investigation.
